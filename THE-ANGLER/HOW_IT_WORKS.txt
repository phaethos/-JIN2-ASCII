HOW IT WORKS - The Angler
phaethos

The whole artwork is one Python file. Almost nothing on the screen is
visible, and that is the point.

1. The lure
   The esca hangs at the tip of a bezier curve, swaying. Its light
   pulses with a slow rhythm plus a fast flicker, and flares at the strike.

2. The light rule
   Every cell computes the lure's light with a quadratic falloff.
   The fish is only drawn where that light is strong enough:
   the body is not a shape on the screen, it is a shape the light
   reveals. The lit water around it is sparse speckles.

3. The fish
   In the fish's own frame it is built from ellipses (body, head,
   pectoral fin) and wedges (tail, dorsal fin, jaws). The tail sways
   by rotating its wedge around a pivot. The jaw rotates open around
   a hinge, upper up, lower down, and the teeth appear along its edges
   only when the mouth opens.

4. The marine snow
   Particles fall slowly and wrap. Their brightness is the lure's light
   where they are, so they glow as they drift through the glow radius.

5. The prey and the strike
   A small fish appears at the right edge and is drawn in toward the
   light, slowing as it arrives. At the strike the jaw snaps shut,
   the lure flares white for a breath, the prey is gone, and the fish
   drifts away into the dark until the loop begins again.

Things you can try (for yourself)
   r0             how far the lure's light reaches
   STRIKE_AT      when the jaw snaps
   LURE_TIP       where the esca hangs
